FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is DuoDesk really free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license, with no paid tier, no ads and no account. The full source is on GitHub.

Which devices can work together?

Any combination: Mac with Windows, Mac with Mac, PC with PC, Linux with anything, and an Android phone or tablet with any computer. With DuoDesk Mesh, all of them at once.

Do my devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. The same Wi-Fi is the easiest, but a USB cable works too, and with no shared network at all DuoDesk connects over Bluetooth or makes a direct Wi-Fi link between the devices.

Does anything go through the cloud?

No. Devices talk only to each other over an encrypted link. There is no account, no relay server and no telemetry.

Why does my computer warn me before opening it?

The apps aren't code-signed with a paid certificate yet, so the system asks once. On macOS, open it, click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. On Android, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps. Updates open normally afterwards.

How is DuoDesk related to TwinDesk?

DuoDesk is a redesigned fork of TwinDesk by Inuka Asith, released under the same GPL-3.0 license. It brings a new interface on top of the same open, local-only foundation.
